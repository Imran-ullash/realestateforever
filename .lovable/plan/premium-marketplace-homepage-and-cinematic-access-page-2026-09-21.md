# Premium marketplace homepage and cinematic access page

## Goal
Move the existing Coming Soon introduction film into the password-protected entry experience, then refine the unlocked homepage into a premium real estate marketplace inspired by DealDeck and InvestorLift while preserving Real Estate Forever’s black, ivory, and gold identity.

## Access page
- Combine the Real Estate Forever branding, original “Something Exceptional Is Coming.” composition, autoplaying muted film, sound control, and password field on `/unlock`.
- Use a cinematic, balanced desktop composition and a clear stacked mobile layout.
- Preserve the existing REF password behavior, error treatment, and secure session flow.

## Homepage
- Remove the Coming Soon film from the unlocked homepage so the property marketplace becomes the immediate focus.
- Keep the current section order and content areas: navigation, main introduction, firm overview, listings, services, reviews, property statement, enquiry, and footer.
- Redesign the opening and listings experience around confident deal discovery: stronger search/filter controls, clear market indicators, concise property metrics, and premium calls to action.
- Refine the supporting sections to feel like one cohesive investment-focused real estate platform rather than a residential showcase.
- Retain the current 38 listings, customer review layout, enquiry flow, imagery, logo, contact details, and brand palette.

## Responsive and quality checks
- Ensure the film and password form remain usable without overlap on desktop and mobile.
- Verify unlocking redirects to the homepage, video autoplay works muted, listing filters work, and no horizontal overflow or browser errors appear.
- Keep unique page metadata for both the access page and homepage.

## Technical notes
- Reuse the existing WebM-first/MP4-fallback video assets and sound toggle.
- Keep authentication logic unchanged; changes are limited to page presentation and client-side interactions.
- Use existing semantic colors and shared controls throughout.
